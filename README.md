## Criteria

Tools on this list should ideally:

1. Have a clear, non-fishy privacy policy — nothing shady going on with your data.
2. Be easy, simple, seamless, and clean to use.
3. Be primarily websites. Command-line tools and downloadable software are nice-to-haves.
4. Be open source.
5. Have no ads (public-good tools).
6. Offer at least two options per category where possible.

Entries that don't fully meet these criteria (e.g. unclear privacy policy, not open source) are marked in _italics_.
